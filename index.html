Questions people ask

What actually happens on a distance Reiki call?

We meet on Zoom and talk for a few minutes about what you’re carrying. Then you get comfy on your own couch while I do the energy work, with crystals and a soft hearth soundscape. Nothing to do but rest. We check in at the end on what came up.

Do I need to believe in any of this for it to work?

Nope. You just have to show up and be open to resting for a minute. Skeptics are welcome here. Some of my favorite sessions started with “I don’t really get this, but okay.”

What if I cry?

Then you cry. That’s not a problem to fix, it’s usually a sign something needed out. Keep tissues nearby. Zero judgment here, ever.

What if I don’t know what I want to talk about?

That’s most people, honestly. “I’m tired and I don’t know why” is a perfectly good place to start. We’ll find the thread together.

What’s the difference between coaching, Reiki and The Mindset Shift?

Hearth Coaching (60 minutes) is talking it through: the dilemma, the stuck spot, the thing you keep putting off. Hearth Reiki (45 minutes) is energy work, mostly quiet, for when you need to rest more than talk. The Mindset Shift (25 minutes) is a short, focused reset for one thing that’s been living rent-free in your head.

Can I do this while my kids are home?

Yes. I’m a mom too, so I get it. If someone needs a snack mid-session, go get the snack. We’ll pick right back up.

Is this therapy?

No. I’m a certified life coach and Reiki Master, not a therapist or a medical provider. I don’t diagnose or treat anything. If what you’re carrying needs a therapist, I’ll tell you honestly and help you find the right person.

What if I need to cancel?

Life happens, especially with kids. If you need to reschedule, just message me and we’ll find a new time. The only thing I ask: if you don’t show up and don’t message me, the session fee is kept, because that time was held just for you.

When are sessions?

Weekdays, on Zoom, Eastern time.

Which one should I book first?

If you’re not sure, start with Hearth Coaching. It’s marked “start here” for a reason. We’ll figure out together whether Reiki or a Mindset Shift makes sense after that.
